## ⚡ What is Mario Kart Nitro?

**Mario Kart Nitro** is a community-driven Mario Kart Wii project built around competitive racing, custom content, online play and a growing racing community.

Nitro takes the foundation of **Mario Kart Wii** and builds a new experience around it — with its own identity, racing environment, tracks, events and online community.

> **Built for racers. Made for the community.**

---

## 🏁 Race Your Way

Mario Kart Nitro is designed around the feeling that made Mario Kart Wii special while giving the game a fresh competitive environment.

### 🌐 Online Racing

Race with players from around the world and compete for your place on the Nitro rankings.

### 🏆 VR & Rankings

Every race can be part of the climb.

Build your **Versus Rating**, move through the ranks and prove yourself against the Nitro community.

**Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond → Master → Grandmaster → Immortal → One Above All**

### 🛣️ Custom Track Experience

Explore the current Nitro track configuration and race through a growing selection of cups and courses.

**24 Cups · 96 Tracks**

### 🎉 Community Events

Nitro also features special racing events throughout the year, including seasonal events and VR multipliers.

## 🎮 Requirements

Mario Kart Nitro is based on **Mario Kart Wii**.

You will need:

* A legally obtained copy of **Mario Kart Wii**
* A compatible Wii / Dolphin setup
* The current Mario Kart Nitro release
* An internet connection for online features

> Mario Kart Wii game files are **not distributed with this repository**.
